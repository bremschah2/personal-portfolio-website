Interview questions, answered

Questions hiring managers ask me

Straight answers to the questions I'm usually asked for IT Project Manager roles.

Why hire a technical project manager?

Data projects fail when nobody on the management side understands the technical risk. I can tell when an estimate is optimistic, when a dependency is fragile, and when a 'small change' isn't small, and I can explain all of that to business stakeholders.

Tell us about a time the plan broke.

Midway through my credit-risk platform, both planned databases were blocked by the cloud provider. I logged it as an issue, compared the alternatives, recorded the decision in ADR-001 and re-planned the downstream phases. The project still finished in scope and at zero cost.

How do you manage risk?

I keep a living RAID log from day one and review it at each phase gate. Any decision with long-term consequences gets an ADR, so the reasoning survives after the meeting ends.

How do you report progress?

With short, regular updates built around working output, not slides: what was delivered, what's at risk, and what I need from stakeholders.

How do you work with remote, multicultural teams?

I work in four languages, write things down, and plan around overlapping hours. Clear written scope and decisions matter more in remote teams than in any office.
