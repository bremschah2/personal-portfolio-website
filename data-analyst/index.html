Interview questions, answered

Questions hiring managers ask me

Straight answers to the questions I'm usually asked for Data Analyst roles.

Why should we hire you as a Data Analyst?

Because I cover the whole path. I can get the data, clean it, analyse it, and build a dashboard that answers a real business question. And because I check that the numbers are right before anyone acts on them. In my credit-risk project, that meant catching overconfident model outputs before they distorted a $197M portfolio view.

What's an insight you're proud of?

That ranking loans by risk alone sends a collections team to the wrong place. A 30% risk on a $2,000 loan matters less than a 15% risk on a $35,000 loan. Ranking by expected dollar loss instead changed the whole priority list.

How do you handle stakeholders who keep asking for 'one more chart'?

I go back to the decision the report supports and agree the KPIs up front. If a new chart doesn't help that decision, it goes into a backlog rather than cluttering the main view.

How do you make sure your numbers are correct?

I reconcile totals against the source, sanity-check extremes, and look for results that are too good to be true. Calibration analysis is one example: it showed my model's probabilities couldn't be taken at face value.

Can you work with our time zone and team?

Yes. I work remotely, with hours that overlap Europe, the Gulf and US East, and I communicate fluently in English, French, Arabic and Russian.
