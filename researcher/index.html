Interview questions, answered

Questions hiring managers ask me

Straight answers to the questions I'm usually asked for Researcher roles.

What is your research about?

My PhD at Ural Federal University's Graduate School of Economics and Management is in Management of Organizational Systems: how organisations are structured, make decisions and use information.

Why hire a researcher with industry experience?

Because I can move a project from question to working evidence without waiting for a separate engineering team. I've built the data pipelines, models and dashboards myself.

How do you make your work reproducible?

Everything is version-controlled, dependencies are pinned, and each major methodological decision is written down with its reasoning, the same discipline I use for ADRs in engineering.

Are you open to international and development research?

Yes. I'm particularly interested in roles with international organisations and research institutes, where my languages and Africa/MENA background add value.
