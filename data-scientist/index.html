Interview questions, answered

Questions hiring managers ask me

Straight answers to the questions I'm usually asked for Data Scientist roles.

Why should we hire you as a Data Scientist?

I take a model all the way from feature analysis to a calibrated, deployed API, and I translate its output into money. I'm also honest about where it stops working, which saves your business from acting on numbers it shouldn't trust.

Your ROC-AUC is 0.696. Isn't that low?

For a production credit scorecard it is, and I say so. The public dataset has no credit-score field, which is usually the strongest predictor. That's why I scoped the model to relative ranking and collections prioritisation, not approve-or-deny decisions, and documented this in ADR-004.

Why did calibration matter so much?

My outputs get multiplied by dollar exposure. An overconfident 60% that is really 30% doubles the expected loss on exactly the loans a team would chase first. Calibration left the ranking unchanged but made the dollar figures trustworthy.

How do you choose features?

I start from the business process and data availability at prediction time, to avoid leakage. Then I use feature importance and permutation importance to keep what genuinely adds signal.

What would you do next with this model?

Champion/challenger testing against a gradient-boosted model, drift monitoring with scheduled recalibration, and adding bureau credit features where available.
