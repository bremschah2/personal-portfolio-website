Interview questions, answered

Questions hiring managers ask me

Straight answers to the questions I'm usually asked for Data Engineer roles.

Why should we hire you as a Data Engineer?

I've built and operated a complete pipeline myself, from ingestion through storage, scheduling and containerised serving, and kept it running in production. I also bring the analyst's and data scientist's view of how the data will be used, which leads to better schemas.

Tell us about a design decision you made under pressure.

My first two database choices, Aurora DSQL and Aurora PostgreSQL Serverless, were both blocked at the AWS account level. I moved to DynamoDB, redesigned the data as a single-table NoSQL schema, and recorded the trade-offs in ADR-001 and ADR-002. The constraint ended up producing a cleaner design.

How do you make pipelines incremental and safe to re-run?

I store a watermark of the last processed batch in DynamoDB itself. Each scheduled run reads it, loads only what's new, and advances it, so a failed run can simply be run again.

How do you control cloud costs?

I make cost a design input. I chose services with a permanent free tier, used on-demand capacity, and picked a scheduled export over a paid live BI connection. The whole platform costs nothing to run.

What would you add for a production setting?

Infrastructure as code, monitoring and alerting on pipeline failures, data quality checks at ingestion, and authentication and rate limiting on the public API.
